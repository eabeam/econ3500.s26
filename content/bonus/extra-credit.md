# Extra credit

As we've discussed in class, you earn up to **two extra credit points** on your final grade. These are added directly to your final course percentage.

## Working sessions

Attending working sessions with Rae is the primary way to earn flex points. You need to attend **8 sessions** (out of the total offered) to receive full credit for this component. **Note that this is a reduction from the previous requirement.**

The formula is: $\min\left(\frac{\text{sessions attended}}{8} \times 2,\ 1\right)$

## Research paper extra credit

There are two additional extra credit opportunities tied to your research paper to encourage you to explore new tools (LaTeX!) and data practices (replicability!)

1. **LaTeX**: If your final paper and tables are written and formatted in LaTeX, you are eligible for up to 1 point.

2. **"Push-button" replication**: If I can open one master do-file, run it, and it produces all of your tables with minimal effort and no changes on my end, you are eligible for up to 1 additional point. The amount depends on how cleanly and completely the replication runs.

Rae and I are both happy to help with LaTeX and replication workflow questions. For either and both, I consider AI use acceptable, provided that you disclose that use clearly in your AI attribution statement.

If some of this does not make much sense yet, that is fine — it will likely be more relevant in April when you are closer to the final paper. You are welcome to reach out now or later in the semester.

## Maximum

The maximum across all activities is **2 points total**.
